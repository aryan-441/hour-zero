HOUR ZERO · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes information handled when you visit and play Hour Zero at zerohourgame.web.app.

Game progress saved on your device

Hour Zero saves a local game profile in browser-managed storage through Godot's web runtime. It can include games played, challenge totals (including questions answered and correct answers), response-time totals, credits and perk inventory, recent question identifiers, best score and depth, run outcomes, and your sound setting. This information is used to maintain your progress and show your personal statistics.

The profile is stored for this browser on this device; it is not synced to an Hour Zero account or sent to a game server by the game code. Clearing this site's browser data can erase it. Private browsing, browser settings, or changing browsers/devices may prevent it from persisting or make it unavailable.

Information handled by hosting

The website is served by Firebase Hosting. Firebase says Hosting processes IP addresses from incoming requests to detect abuse and provide usage analysis, and retains IP data for a few months. See Firebase privacy and security information for details. Google's handling of this information is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising, and cookies

Hour Zero uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. Google Analytics may collect page and interaction events, session statistics, approximate location, and browser and device information. Standard Google Analytics 4 website tags use first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visitors and sessions; the exact events and storage depend on the site's Analytics configuration. Google says GA4 uses IP addresses at collection time to derive location and discards raw IP addresses before data is logged. Read what Google Analytics collects and its cookie documentation. The current game source does not include account sign-in or an advertising integration.

Changes to this policy
