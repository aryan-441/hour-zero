About Hour Zero

Hour Zero is a browser game of timed math, pattern, logic, memory, and attention challenges, set inside a high-stakes network infiltration.

Think fast, manage your run, and get out before your signal is found. The game runs in your browser and is designed for desktop keyboard play.
