About Hour Zero

Hour Zero is a single-player, high-risk, high-reward game that puts your mental capabilities to the test. Infiltrate a secure digital network by solving timed math, pattern, logic, memory, and attention challenges.

One more signal?

Each correct answer earns points and adds credits to your unbanked cache. As your exposure rises, the network becomes more likely to detect you. After each resolved challenge, choose whether to push deeper for greater rewards or extract to secure your credits.

If you are detected, the run ends and your unbanked cache is lost. Credits secured in earlier runs stay safe. The pressure comes from knowing when to leave, even when the next challenge could pay more.

Build your next run

Boss challenges test you with multi-step problems. Tactical perks bought with secured credits give you extra ways to manage time, exposure, and rewards. Your stats and personal bests track your progress between runs.

How to play

Play in a laptop browser with a keyboard and mouse. Open How to play from the game menu for detailed instructions about challenges, extraction, exposure, bosses, and perks.

Your progress and sound settings are saved locally in your browser on this device. Hour Zero has no synced account or online competitive leaderboard.
